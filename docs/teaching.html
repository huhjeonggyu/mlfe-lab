Students interested in studying financial mathematics in our lab should be familiar with the following foundations, which are covered in these courses.

  • Basic Financial Mathematics
    Fundamentals of financial markets, including stocks, bonds, futures, and options.
    Related business courses such as Investments or Derivatives may be taken instead.
  • Computational and Applied Mathematics
    Foundations of machine learning and deep learning.
    Machine learning courses offered by statistics, computer science, or electrical engineering departments may be taken instead.
  • Scientific Computing and Deep Learning
    Modern reinforcement learning, traditional control theory, and the integration of control theory and reinforcement learning.
  • Financial Mathematics
    Fundamentals of derivatives, stochastic calculus, and Black–Scholes theory.
  • Advanced Financial Mathematics
    The Merton investment–consumption model and stochastic-control-based deep learning methods, including PG-DPO and Deep BSDE.
