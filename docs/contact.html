Research careers

Our lab supports students pursuing the following career paths.

  • Derivatives modeling and valuation (master’s / PhD): For students aiming to work at bond pricing agencies, securities firms, and other financial institutions.
  • Asset management (master’s / PhD): For students pursuing careers in portfolio and asset management at asset management firms and hedge funds.
  • Academic research (PhD): For students aiming to become researchers or academics in stochastic control and financial engineering.

For further information, please contact jghuh@skku.edu.
